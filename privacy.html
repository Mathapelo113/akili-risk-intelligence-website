PRIVACY & DATA PROTECTION

POPIA Privacy Notice

This notice explains how Akili Risk Intelligence processes personal information when you visit our website, contact us, request services or provide information for an AI risk or governance engagement.

Last updated: 6 October 2026

1. Who we are

Akili Risk Intelligence (“Akili”, “we”, “us” or “our”) provides AI risk assessment, AI governance and related advisory services. For personal information that we determine the purposes and means of processing, Akili acts as the responsible party under the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (“POPIA”).

For privacy enquiries, please contact us at info@akiliriskintelligence.co.za.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect information such as your name, business email address, telephone number, organisation, job title or role, service interests, enquiry details, assessment information, and information you voluntarily provide during a client engagement.

Our website contact form is intended for business enquiries. Please do not submit passwords, financial account details, identity document numbers, health information or other special personal information through the general contact form unless we specifically request and provide an appropriate secure channel.

3. How we use personal information

We may process personal information to respond to enquiries, communicate with prospective or existing clients, provide and administer services, conduct AI risk and governance engagements, prepare reports and recommendations, maintain business records, manage contractual relationships, protect our systems and website, comply with legal obligations, and improve our services.

Where applicable, processing is based on a lawful ground recognised by POPIA, including consent, steps necessary to enter into or perform a contract, compliance with a legal obligation, or our legitimate interests where permitted by law.

4. AI Risk Assessment questionnaire

Our AI Risk Assessment questionnaire is hosted and processed through Tally. Information submitted through that questionnaire is used to understand the organisation’s AI use, risks, governance environment and assessment requirements. The questionnaire contains its own privacy acknowledgement and should be read before submission.

Because Tally is a third-party service provider, information submitted through the questionnaire may be processed by Tally in accordance with its own terms and privacy documentation. We recommend that clients review the relevant Tally privacy information before submitting information.

5. Sharing and service providers

We may use trusted service providers to operate our website, email, forms, cloud storage, business administration and client-delivery workflows. Where another party processes personal information on our behalf, we take reasonable steps to require appropriate confidentiality and security measures.

We do not sell personal information as a business practice. We may disclose information where required by law, to protect rights or security, or where necessary for the delivery and administration of an agreed service.

6. International processing

Some technology providers used by Akili may process information outside South Africa. Where personal information is transferred to or processed in another country, Akili will take steps required by applicable law, including considering the safeguards and circumstances applicable to the transfer.

7. Security

Akili takes reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal information against loss, damage, unauthorised access, unlawful processing and other security risks. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so we also ask users not to submit unnecessary sensitive information through public website forms.

8. Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, to provide services, maintain appropriate business and legal records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, or comply with legal and regulatory obligations. Retention periods may differ depending on the type and purpose of the information.

9. Your rights

Subject to applicable law and any lawful limitations, data subjects may have rights relating to access to, correction of, deletion of, objection to or limitation of processing of their personal information, as well as rights relating to consent where processing is based on consent. Requests can be directed to info@akiliriskintelligence.co.za.

10. Direct marketing

We will not treat an enquiry or assessment submission as automatic permission to send unrelated marketing communications. Where POPIA requires consent for direct electronic marketing, we will seek the appropriate consent or rely on another lawful basis permitted by law.

11. Cookies and website technologies

Our website may use basic technologies required for website functionality and security. If we introduce analytics, advertising or other technologies that materially change how personal information is collected or used, we may update this notice and implement any additional notices or choices required by applicable law.

12. Complaints

If you believe that your personal information has been processed unlawfully, please contact Akili first so that we can investigate and respond. You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa. The Regulator provides eServices for POPIA-related matters, including complaints and security-compromise reporting.
